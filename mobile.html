01 / About

Behind the kit

Stelios Sioulas is a drummer from Thessaloniki, based in Athens, with a career spanning original bands, tribute acts, studio recordings, touring and live collaborations.

Across much of his recorded work, Stelios develops and writes his own drum parts rather than simply performing pre-written drum arrangements. His drum solos and solo sections are his own compositions.
