Privacy & cookies

Statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic and performance statistics. Your browser connects to Cloudflare to load the code and send measurements. According to Cloudflare, its measurement code does not use cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage. Contact form contents are not sent to Analytics.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Third-party videos

Instagram, TikTok and YouTube videos load only when you choose the corresponding load button. Your choice applies only to that video and is not saved as a permanent preference. You can close the player or open the video directly on the service. Once a player loads, its provider may use cookies or other technologies under its own policy.

Contact

When you send a message, your name, email and message are delivered through Formspree so we can read and respond to your enquiry. Please avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Formspree — Privacy

Temporary preferences

We use sessionStorage for temporary functional preferences, such as the audio intro and scroll position when switching language. This storage is distinct from tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts and selected event posters load from an external image provider, which receive the corresponding browser requests. External links are governed by the policy of the service you visit.
