01 / About

Behind the kit

Stelios Sioulas is a drummer from Thessaloniki, Greece, with a career spanning original bands, tribute acts, studio recordings and live collaborations.

His projects include The Prisoners, The Puppets, Sarissa, Star.Gate, Forbidden Lore, Illusive Mirrors, John Jeff Touch, Ούτε Σπόντα, Persona Non Grata, Pop ’N’ Roll, 6 Degrees of Separation, Palermo, Soletaken and Obscurus Rex, alongside additional session and live work.
