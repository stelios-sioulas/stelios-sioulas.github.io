01 / About

Behind the kit

Stelios Sioulas is an Athens-based drummer with a long-running presence across metal, rock, tribute projects and the wider Greek live scene. After building years of musical experience and live collaborations in Thessaloniki, he has continued his work in Athens across bands, live projects and teaching.

Across much of his recorded work, Stelios develops and writes his own drum parts rather than simply performing pre-written drum arrangements. His drum solos and solo sections are his own compositions.

His projects include The Prisoners, Persona Non Grata, Pop ’N’ Roll, Obscurus Rex, Mushroom People, Onion Rings, Sarissa, Star.Gate, Forbidden Lore, Illusive Mirrors, The Puppets, Soletaken and other session and live collaborations.
