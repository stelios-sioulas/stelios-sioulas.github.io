Professional profile

Stelios Sioulas is an Athens-based drummer with long-running experience across metal, rock and tribute projects, live performance, recording work and drum teaching. Across much of his recorded work, he develops and writes his own drum parts, while his drum solos and solo sections are his own compositions.
