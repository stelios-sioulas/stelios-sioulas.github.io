Drum Teaching

Practical drum lessons focused on technique, timing, coordination, groove, fills and the musical skills needed to play with confidence.

Learn to play

Lessons are built around the drummer: developing solid fundamentals, improving coordination and timing, understanding the role of the drums inside a band, and applying exercises directly to real playing situations.

Practical approach

The aim is not simply to repeat exercises. Technique is connected to groove, dynamics, musicality and the ability to use what you practise when you actually play.

Teaching focus

Foundation

Technique

Stick control, hand and foot technique, posture and efficient movement.

Time

Timing & Coordination

Exercises for independence, internal timing and coordination between hands and feet.

Playing

Groove

Building solid grooves and learning how to support the music rather than simply fill space.

Application

Fills

Developing fills that are controlled, musical and connected to the groove.

Performance

Playing in a Band

Dynamics, listening, arrangement awareness and practical live-playing skills.

From Thessaloniki to Athens

A different chapter

Stelios built a strong presence in the Thessaloniki music scene before moving to Athens during the COVID-19 period. The move coincided with a major interruption of live music and teaching activity, making it difficult to establish the same professional network immediately in a new city. His experience and musical background, however, were already developed through years of work with bands, live performances and musicians in Thessaloniki.
